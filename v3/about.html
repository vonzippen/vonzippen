ABOUT

Von Zippen was formed in 2014 in Ticino, when Faze (vocals and piano) and Hedgehog (guitar) joined forces, soon followed by Rocco on drums and Staf on bass. After an early period of cover songs, overdriven amplifiers, and plenty of sweat, the band began writing original material in 2017. The real turning point came in 2019, when the band reinvented itself and embraced sleaze rock, drawing inspiration from acts such as Guns N’ Roses, Mötley Crüe, Ratt, and L.A. Guns.

The result is a raw and straightforward sound: pounding drums, a warm and driving bass, razor-sharp guitars, and choruses made to be shouted from the front row—preferably with something cold in your hand.

In 2024, they released the EP Ain’t Going Down No More, led by the acoustic single Tryin’ So Hard, showcasing both their more reflective side and their unmistakably carefree spirit. This was followed in 2026 by the singles Hit’Em Back and You Have To Run Away, further strengthening a musical identity that blends sleaze rock with heavy-hitting energy.

The band is now based in Zurich. Through live shows, festivals, and radio airplay, Von Zippen continue to stand by a simple idea: rock is more than just a music genre—it’s an attitude. It is life energy, a taste for intensity, and a passion for experiencing things to the fullest. In other words, rock isn’t just something you listen to… it’s something you live.

Von Zippen’s lineup:

Amos “Faze” Malorgio — vocals & piano

Samuele “Hedgehog” Cambianica — guitar

Stefano “Staf” Martini — bass

Rocco Vitale — drums
