THE BAND

Von Zippen presents a 21st century take on 80’s sleaze rock.

The Swiss band follows the path of bands such as GN’R, L.A. Guns, Mötley Crüe and Kix, unleashing the raw and pure energy within us all! Both the band’s stage presence and their EP Ain’t Going Down No More embody the electrifying and irresistible essence of rock ’n’ roll.

Von Zippen’s songs explore instinct, desire, love, greed and social critique. With penetrating guitar riffs, groovy bass lines, in-your-face drum beats, powerful vocals, and the occasional boogie-woogie on the keyboard, we aim to sublimate the deep, basic and powerful desires that lie within us all!

If you crave intensity and adrenaline, give Ain’t Going Down No More a listen and catch us at one of our shows. We look forward to seeing you there! The band regularly hits the stage at pubs and festivals across Switzerland.

Von Zippen’s lineup:

Amos “Faze” Malorgio — vocals & piano

Samuele “Hedgehog” Cambianica — guitar

Stefano “Staf” Martini — bass
